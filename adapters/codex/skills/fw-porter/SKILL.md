# Five forces, evidence first

For each force (rivalry, new entrants, substitutes, buyer power, supplier power) write: a 1-5 pressure score, the two facts that justify it, and what would change it. A force with no sourced fact stays unscored and says "no evidence".

- Rivalry: count real competitors serving the same job; note pricing pages, funding, growth signals.
- New entrants: what stops a competent team from copying this in 3 months? Be honest if the answer is nothing.
- Substitutes: include "do nothing", spreadsheets, agencies, and incumbents' bundled features.
- Buyer power: switching cost, concentration of buyers, procurement friction.
- Supplier power: dependence on one API, platform, or data source. Platform risk counts.

Finish with the single force that most threatens margins and a test that would show whether it bites. Tag every claim.
