# Pricing

- Anchor from three sides: value delivered to the customer (hours saved x rate, losses avoided), competitor prices (open their pricing pages), and your cost floor with a minimum margin.
- Propose 2 or 3 price points and the test for each: a pre-sale, a priced landing page, or a Van Westendorp survey with at least 30 target respondents.
- Check payment fees, taxes, refunds and support time in unit economics.
- Prefer simple tiers. State which segment each tier is for and what would make a customer upgrade.
- Pricing is a hypothesis until money moves. Label it so.
